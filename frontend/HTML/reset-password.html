How the reset works

Three steps, in this order.

1
Open the emailed link

The link contains a short-lived, single-use token. Once it has been used or has expired, it cannot be reused.

2
Set a new password

It must meet every rule listed on the left. The new password replaces the expired or forgotten one immediately.

3
Sign in with two factors

Enter the new password, then the one-time code sent to your registered email address.

Password and account rules

  • A password can only be changed after one day of use
  • Passwords expire after 60 days — sign-in is blocked until reset
  • Resets use a short-lived, single-use link sent to your email
  • Repeated failed attempts are rate-limited
The token is sent over HTTPS and never placed in a URL that is logged or shared. Your new password is stored hashed and salted, never in plain text.
