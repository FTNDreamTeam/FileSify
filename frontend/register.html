What happens after you register

Three steps, in this order.

1
Confirm your email address

A confirmation link is sent to the address you entered. Registration is not complete until it is opened.

2
Your default quota is assigned

Every new account receives a default storage quota. Every upload is checked against it, and you can request an increase later.

3
Sign in with two factors

After confirming, sign in with your password and the one-time code that is sent to your email address.

Password rules

  • Can only be changed after one day of use
  • Expires after 60 days — sign-in is blocked until reset
  • Reset uses a short-lived link sent to your email
