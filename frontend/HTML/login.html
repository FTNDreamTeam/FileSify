How sign-in works

Three steps, in this order.

1
Enter your password

Password alone is not enough — it is only the first factor.

2
Enter the one-time code

A short-lived code is sent to your registered email address. Both factors must match.

3
You are signed in

Only after both factors are verified does the session start and the dashboard open.

Password and account rules

  • Passwords expire after 60 days — sign-in is blocked until reset
  • A password can only be changed after one day of use
  • Reset uses a short-lived, single-use link sent to your email
  • Repeated failed attempts are rate-limited
Credentials are sent over HTTPS. Successful and failed sign-in attempts are logged for audit, without passwords or sensitive data.
